Overview

Local-first habit tracking

Daily Routine: Habit Widget is a personal habit and routine tracker designed to keep your records on your device whenever possible.

Data you create

Habit and routine records

The app may store habits, routines, completion history, streaks, goal progress, reminder settings, widget preferences, appearance settings, and onboarding choices locally on your device.

Widgets and Watch

Shared app group data

To keep widgets, Live Activities, and Apple Watch views current, selected habit titles, routine titles, progress values, completion state, and widget settings may be written to the app's shared container on your device.

Notifications

Optional reminders

If you enable reminders, the app uses iOS notification permission to schedule habit reminders and snooze actions. You can change notification access in iOS Settings.

Accounts

No app account required

The app does not require a user account, and this site does not provide account registration.

Analytics and ads

No behavioral ad profile

The app is not designed to sell your habit records or build a behavioral advertising profile from your routines.

Purchases

Apple handles billing

If paid features or subscriptions are offered, purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Daily Routine does not receive or store your full payment card details.

Deletion

Control local app data

You can remove local app data by deleting the app from your device. Apple-managed purchase records may remain available through your Apple ID.
