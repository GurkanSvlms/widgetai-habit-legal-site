Widgets

Refresh the app first

If a widget does not update, open the app once, complete or edit a habit, and check that the habit or routine is enabled for widgets.

Reminders

Check iOS notification access

If reminders are missing, confirm that notifications are allowed for Habitile in iOS Settings and that the habit still has a reminder time.

Subscriptions

Apple manages billing

Subscriptions, refunds, cancellation, renewal, and purchase history are managed by Apple through your Apple ID and App Store account settings.

Privacy and data

Local-first records

Most records are stored locally on your device or in the shared app group used by widgets and Watch features. You can remove local app data by deleting the app.
